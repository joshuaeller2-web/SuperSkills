# business-ops

Choose the requested mode: offer/content, prospect research, operations or data-handling review. Read existing business context when present; ask only for missing details that change the deliverable. Do not create new business accounts or infrastructure automatically.

For an offer, state audience, outcome, exact deliverable, price assumptions, client inputs, exclusions and delivery conditions. Add observable acceptance checks for each deliverable, such as reconciled balances, exception counts, and a documented unresolved-items list when those fit the work. For prospect research, cite evidence for budget, authority, need and timeline; mark unknowns rather than inventing a numeric qualification score. Use MEDDIC fields when deal complexity warrants them. Identify a concrete reason to contact the prospect, then draft truthful concise outreach. Sending requires the user's authorization.

For an SOP, name owner, trigger, tools and steps. Each step has an actor, observable pass condition and failure branch. Include intake inputs, acceptance, reviewer and handoff. Keep client data in its authorized workspace; respect existing privacy and retention rules. Do not promise revenue, ranking, AI citations or regulatory outcomes.

For SEO content, make factual passages self-contained, sources clear and headings useful. Inspect the rendered page and actual crawler settings before making technical claims. Respect the user's crawler policy; do not indiscriminately unblock training crawlers.

Read [draft provenance and incorporated methods](references/draft-methods.md) when revising these methods.
