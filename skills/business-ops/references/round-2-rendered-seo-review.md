# Rendered seo review

Verify a claimed SEO defect against the actual rendered/live page and relevant response or metadata. Investigate empty or failed crawls before concluding the page has no content. Distinguish unavailable ranking data from poor rankings. Verify product and pricing claims against current sources, and prioritize an evidenced action with its expected check.

Use available authorized browsing and audit tools. The source's MCP, credit purchase, account creation and product-specific templates are not inherited requirements. A successful render alone is not evidence of indexing, ranking or AI citation improvement.
