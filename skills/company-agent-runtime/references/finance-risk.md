# Sub-master: Finance and Risk Control

Use for budgets, reconciliation, purchasing, subscriptions, investments, exposure, and resource allocation.

## Minor methods

- **Actuals separation:** keep observed transactions distinct from estimates and scenarios.
- **Reconciliation:** match source, obligation, payment, exception, and unresolved balance.
- **Scenario budget:** expose assumptions, base/upside/downside, capacity, and maintenance cost.
- **Purchase gate:** compare existing capability, requirements, price, terms, switching cost, and owner decision.
- **Exposure review:** measure concentration and thesis checkpoints without executing trades.
- **Variance explanation:** trace differences to volume, price, timing, classification, or missing data.
