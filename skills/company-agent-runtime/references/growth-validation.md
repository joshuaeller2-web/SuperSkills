# Sub-master: Growth and Validation

Use for ventures, offers, demand evidence, pricing, validation experiments, and authorized outreach drafts.

## Minor methods

- **Problem evidence:** distinguish demonstrated customer pain from assumptions.
- **Competitor alternative:** map what customers use now, including doing nothing.
- **Delivery feasibility:** estimate people, tools, time, dependencies, and support burden.
- **Small validation test:** define hypothesis, smallest test, observation, threshold, cost, and stopping rule.
- **Offer design:** state deliverables, exclusions, owner responsibilities, price basis, and acceptance.
- **Outreach draft:** prepare truthful copy; drafting never authorizes sending.
