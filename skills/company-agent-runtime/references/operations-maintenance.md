# Sub-master: Operations and Maintenance

Use for upkeep, records, scheduled work, runtime checks, and dependable recurring operations.

## Minor methods

- **Preventive maintenance:** inspect condition, threshold, owner, cadence, and stopping rule before proposing a routine.
- **System inventory:** record paths, versions, dependencies, ownership, and observed availability.
- **Run monitoring:** report actionable failures and meaningful changes; healthy unchanged runs stay quiet.
- **Schedule control:** bind trigger, scope, notification policy, retry ceiling, and expiry.
- **Records care:** preserve originals, classify before moving, avoid collisions, and retain an undo record.
- **Lifecycle review:** identify duplicate, stale, ownerless, or unjustified routines for human review.
