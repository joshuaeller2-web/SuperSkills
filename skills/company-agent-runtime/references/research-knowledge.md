# Sub-master: Research and Knowledge

Use for source discovery, extraction, synthesis, knowledge maintenance, learning, and retrieval.

## Minor methods

- **Question framing:** define the decision, evidence threshold, time range, and exclusions.
- **Source eligibility:** distinguish approved, primary, supporting, stale, conflicting, and unavailable sources.
- **Evidence extraction:** preserve claim, passage, date, version, and source link.
- **Contradiction analysis:** show disagreement and missing evidence rather than forcing consensus.
- **Grounded synthesis:** separate supported findings, contested claims, inference, and uncertainty.
- **Retrieval evaluation:** test representative questions against expected source support.
