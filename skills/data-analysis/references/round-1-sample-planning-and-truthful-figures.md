# Sample planning and truthful figures

For sample planning, record study design, meaningful effect, alpha, desired power and allocation ratio. Distinguish per-group from total sample size and round required enrollment upward. Use an available validated calculation method and retain its inputs and outputs. A separate executable calculator was not established by source review; do not claim one is installed. After collection, report precision and sensitivity rather than observed-effect post-hoc power.

For figures, preserve raw data and transformations, label interval meaning and replication unit, and distinguish missing, zero, censored and excluded observations. Disclose transformed axes and smoothing. Use redundant visual encodings and a data alternative. Inspect the exported artifact at its delivery size. Verify publisher-specific requirements separately.
