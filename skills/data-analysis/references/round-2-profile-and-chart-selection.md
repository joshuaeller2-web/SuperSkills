# Profile and chart selection

Profile format, columns, row counts, inferred types, missingness, cardinality and frequency before choosing a chart. Preserve numeric identifiers and leading zeros; identify uncertain type inference and confirm it from meaning, not only parse success.

Match the question to a trend, comparison, distribution or relationship plot. High cardinality does not prove continuous measurement. Skewness alone does not justify a log scale, and one bimodality coefficient is not proof of two populations. Show units, transformations and missing-data handling. Use available tools; qsv is optional and not installed by this method.
