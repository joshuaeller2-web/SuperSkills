# Editorial check

Check for definite subjects, active verbs, concrete language and parallel constructions. Remove words that do no work while preserving the meaning, sequence, evidence and required terminology. Prefer a clear sentence over a mechanical banned-word substitution. Apply a writing check when revising prose, not as a reason to truncate the task or omit uncertainty.
