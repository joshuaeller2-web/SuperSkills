# Super Investment Research

Combine business analysis, market evidence, and risk discipline while keeping their time horizons distinct.

## Route the question

| Mode | Main question |
| --- | --- |
| Company and valuation | What drives cash generation, what can impair it, and what expectations does the price imply? |
| Market or trading setup | What measurable condition creates the setup, over what horizon, and what invalidates it? |
| Portfolio risk | What exposures, concentrations, liquidity needs, and downside scenarios matter? |
| Backtest | Does a precisely specified strategy survive realistic costs and out-of-sample evaluation? |

Use the requested mode rather than producing every analysis for every ticker. Read [research methods](references/methods.md) for valuation, risk calculations, or backtests. A technically attractive setup does not prove long-term value, and a strong business does not establish a timely entry.

## Establish reliable inputs

Confirm the security, exchange, currency, decision horizon, and data cutoff. Resolve share classes and ADR differences before comparison. For current claims, retrieve current primary filings, issuer releases, exchange information, or available market data. Never present cached or remembered prices as live.

Record dates, units, periods, data delay, and sources for material figures. Reconcile conflicting numbers using definitions and reporting periods; do not average mismatched figures. Missing input remains unavailable, not zero. Distinguish facts, forecasts, assumptions, and inference.

## Analyze and challenge

For a company, connect customers and revenue drivers to unit economics, competitive durability, reinvestment, management incentives, cash conversion, debt, and dilution. Assess business quality separately from price. When valuation matters, show at least a bounded base case and decision-relevant alternative tied to explicit cash-flow assumptions. If figures are missing, state the scenario inputs and reconciliation needed before calculation rather than omitting the valuation plan or inventing values. Name the operating or financial evidence, including persistent cash-conversion weakness when relevant, that would invalidate the thesis. Sparse public information lowers evidence confidence; it does not automatically establish a poor business.

For a trade, specify the instrument, horizon, entry/exit conditions, invalidation, liquidity, event risks, and costs. Use actually retrieved price/volume data for technical claims.

For a portfolio, use positions and constraints the user supplies or authorizes access to. Do not invent account balances, risk tolerance, tax status, or loss capacity. Give conditional scenarios when personalization inputs are missing.

Challenge the preferred thesis with its strongest countercase and the evidence that would change it. Avoid celebrity impersonation or implying that a checklist guarantees returns.

## Deliver

Lead with the research conclusion and its principal uncertainty. Show only the valuation, scenarios, catalysts, or risks needed for the question, with source links close to material claims. Label historical simulations and their limits.

This skill includes local calculations from supplied inputs; it does not provide market feeds, a backtest engine or brokerage credentials. Use available tools honestly. It does not authorize orders, transfers, account changes, or paid data purchases.

Read [sources](references/sources.md) only for provenance or revision.
