# Valuation cross checks

Start with a dated, sourced company snapshot and reconcile units and periods. For DCF, state cash-flow, growth, discount and terminal assumptions, terminal-value contribution and sensitivity ranges. Explain peer selection for relative valuation. For consensus, date the observations and show dispersion and stale/missing coverage.

Compare valuation methods and explain disagreement rather than averaging incompatible results. Distinguish sourced observations from model assumptions and calculations. The upstream named finance/data tools are not installed by this reference. Missing market data remains missing; tool-returned figures still need source and arithmetic checks. Research is not authorization to trade.
