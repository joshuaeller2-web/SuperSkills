# Super Job Search

Connect verified candidate experience to suitable, current opportunities without inventing qualifications or losing track of applications.

## Establish the candidate facts

Use the supplied resume, profile, preferences, and prior answers. Separate hard requirements from preferences: role, seniority, location/work mode, compensation and currency, work authorization, and industry constraints. Unknown is not a failed requirement and must not be silently filled in.

Keep preferences distinct from employment history when drafting: requiring remote work does not mean the candidate currently works remotely, and years using a tool do not establish years in a particular job title. Use the supplied fact at its original level of specificity rather than turning it into an unsupported personal claim.

Ask only for missing information needed by the present task. Research can proceed while a nonblocking preference is unresolved; a required application answer cannot be guessed. Do not turn assumptions about remote work or technical roles into universal search defaults.

## Find and qualify

Search available sources and verify shortlisted postings on employer or ATS pages when possible. Record role, company, location, link, posting/check date, salary basis if published, and fit evidence. Third-party aggregators are discovery sources; verify their coverage before relying on them for a country or profession.

Deduplicate by employer and requisition identifier, then canonical URL where available. Compare against the existing application tracker. Preserve separate requisitions even when titles match. Separate unavailable postings from confirmed closed ones.

Use a small comparison table: match, meaningful gap, unresolved requirement, next action. Avoid arbitrary precision in match percentages; rank by documented must-haves and supporting experience.

## Prepare the application

Map relevant job requirements to evidence from the candidate's history. Tailor emphasis and vocabulary while preserving dates, employers, qualifications, metrics, and actual responsibilities. Do not keyword-stuff or create missing achievements.

Produce the requested resume changes, cover letter, referral draft, or interview practice. Interview examples must be based on real experience or clearly marked practice scenarios. Keep the original resume intact and distinguish derived versions.

## Submit and track only within authorization

Searching, matching, and drafting do not authorize contacting a recruiter or submitting an application. When submission is authorized, check the exact employer, posting, files, form answers, and current batch limits before sending. Ask only about missing answers or actions outside that authorization.

If the site requires a human authentication or CAPTCHA step, pause that action and continue independent preparation. After submission, retain the actual confirmation and set the tracker to submitted only on observed success. Otherwise use drafted, prepared, or submission-unverified. Never infer success from clicking a button alone.

Use an existing tracker if one is provided; create a simple task-local tracker when tracking is requested. Do not install a database or impose an upstream browser wrapper.
