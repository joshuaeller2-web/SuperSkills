# Scenario design

## Minimum specification

1. Decision and time horizon.
2. Seed sources, dates, digests, and known gaps.
3. Population size, archetypes, relationships, and information boundaries.
4. Environment rules and initial state.
5. Baseline, interventions, and controlled variables.
6. Number of rounds, replications, seeds, stop rules, and budget.
7. Observable outcome measures and failure conditions.

Use a small pilot before a large population or long horizon. A pilot checks whether the rules produce interpretable events, whether an intervention reaches the intended actors, and whether logs contain enough evidence to reconstruct a result.

If the prompt asks what one expert thinks, route to `super-decision-council`. If the task estimates a time series or treatment effect from observed measurements, route to `data-analysis`. Use this skill when interaction and emergent behavior are the question.
