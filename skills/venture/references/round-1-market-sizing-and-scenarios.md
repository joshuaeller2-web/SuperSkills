# Market sizing and scenarios

Fix the market boundary, period, unit and denominator before estimating size. Track claim, source and assumption identifiers. Compare independent top-down and bottom-up estimates; prevent overlapping segments or value-chain double counting. Reconcile differences and derive conditional serviceable and obtainable markets with sensitivity scenarios.

For a what-if question, state the changed variable, magnitude, horizon and starting conditions. Compare a baseline with relevant upside, downside and surprises; state assumptions, observable triggers, consequences and robust responses. Choose the useful branch count instead of claiming exhaustive coverage. The reviewed source uses 4–6 branches and a shorter quick mode, not a fixed five. Do not invent probabilities or apply its unsupported golden-ratio allocation rule.
